## Laptop Pricing & Product Feature Analytics | Python

## Data Science

### **Python | Pandas | NumPy | EDA | Statistical Analysis | Pricing Analytics | Business Intelligence**

### Project Overview

Developed a Python-based Laptop Pricing Analytics project to investigate laptop prices, hardware specifications, product features, customer ratings, and pricing patterns across different laptop brands and configurations.

Applied data cleaning, preprocessing, Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA), descriptive statistics, feature analysis, and data visualization to identify relationships between laptop specifications and product pricing.

The project aims to generate business-relevant insights that support product benchmarking, pricing evaluation, and data-driven purchasing decisions.

## Business Problem

Laptop manufacturers, retailers, and customers need to understand how product specifications influence pricing and market positioning.

Different hardware configurations, brands, and product characteristics may be associated with significant differences in laptop prices.

This project investigates these relationships to support evidence-based product comparisons, pricing assessments, and market analysis.

## Project Objectives

- Analyze laptop pricing across brands and product categories.
- Investigate relationships between hardware specifications and prices.
- Examine processor, RAM, storage, and graphics configurations.
- Evaluate laptop operating systems and product characteristics.
- Analyze customer ratings and their relationship with pricing.
- Identify pricing distributions, variations, and potential outliers.
- Generate actionable insights for product benchmarking and market positioning.

## Project Highlights

1. **Data Cleaning & Preprocessing:** Prepared laptop product records using Python and Pandas by examining missing values, validating data types, standardizing formats, and improving dataset consistency.
2. **Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA):** Investigated laptop brands, processors, RAM, storage, operating systems, graphics specifications, prices, and customer ratings.
3. **Laptop Pricing Analysis:** Examined price distributions and differences across laptop brands and hardware configurations.
4. **Hardware Specification Analysis:** Evaluated processor characteristics, RAM capacity, storage specifications, and graphics configurations to understand their relationships with laptop pricing.
5. **Brand & Product Segment Analysis:** Compared laptop brands and configurations to explore pricing differences and market positioning.
6. **Feature Engineering & Analysis:** Organized numerical and categorical product features to support structured statistical comparisons.
7. **Statistical Analysis:** Applied descriptive statistics and distribution analysis to examine price variation, feature characteristics, and potential outliers.
8. **Customer Rating Analysis:** Investigated customer ratings alongside laptop specifications and prices to explore patterns in product feedback.
9. **Data Visualization & Reporting:** Used Matplotlib and Seaborn to communicate laptop price distributions, feature comparisons, brand-level patterns, and rating relationships.
10. **Business Insight Generation:** Interpreted analytical findings to support pricing evaluation, product benchmarking, and specification-based purchasing decisions.
11. **Machine Learning Data Preparation:** Prepared structured product data that may support future laptop price prediction modeling following appropriate feature selection, training, and validation.
